# 🌱 Open Learning Notes

Open notes on learning, art, and educational design.

This repository gathers working notes, essays, and experiments
around learning as an experience rather than a product.

It is not a course.  
It is not a method.  
It is not a platform.

---

## 🧭 Why this exists

Most educational projects optimise for speed, efficiency,
measurable outcomes, or scalability.

These notes explore a different direction:

- 🎨 learning as perception  
- 🌱 learning as curiosity  
- 🤝 learning as shared experience  
- 🕰️ learning as something that unfolds over time  

The goal is not optimisation, but understanding.

---

## 📂 What you will find here

- **📝 Notes**  
  Short texts documenting decisions, questions, and design choices.

- **📘 Essays**  
  Longer reflections connecting pedagogy, art, and learning design.

- **🧪 Experiments**  
  Descriptions of tools, spaces, or approaches tested in practice.

Everything here is incomplete by design.

---

## 🚫 What this repository deliberately avoids

- growth optimisation  
- marketing language  
- promises of fast results  
- behavioural manipulation  
- rigid methodologies  

This is not an EdTech startup.

---

## 👀 How to read this repository

There is no fixed order.

Start anywhere.  
Read slowly.  
Follow what resonates.  
Ignore the rest.

These notes are written primarily as documentation.
They are shared openly for those who may find them useful.
